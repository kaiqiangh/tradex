- The standalone prototype contacts no real Codex, CLIProxyAPI, broker/exchange, or market-data backend and performs no real credential/archive/database I/O.
- Futures, A-share integration, and additional providers retain their existing future scope.
- These runtime boundaries are separate from the interaction defects above; fixture limitations do not explain away FAILED behavior.
- A status change requires a new source revision and reproducible evidence, synchronized with the Chinese matrix. A specification clarification alone cannot upgrade a result to passing.
