The pre-trade `account.commission` response documents standard, tax and special
rates plus the BNB discount flags, `discountAsset`, and discount multiplier. It
does not declare the asset that a future fill will actually debit. The same
official page's `myTrades` response includes `commission` and `commissionAsset`
for completed trades. This confirms the RevC distinction: the pre-trade current
fee model is a scenario, while the actual fill asset is post-trade evidence.

The support guidance describes received-asset fees by default, discounted BNB
when BNB deduction applies, original fees when BNB is insufficient, and warns
that the pre-fill estimate is reference-only because final filled amounts are
not known. This supports considering a disclosed conditional-outcome set, but it
is neither a unique pre-fill fee-asset declaration nor a guaranteed fee cap.

AC5 already has public evidence for the exact BASE/QUOTE/BNB rows, original
free/locked values, the account-read start/first-receipt/digest, account and
Proposal binding, and retirement on missing/duplicate rows. The public
`owning_bnb_fee_estimate_uses_continuous_market_and_compares_the_complete_discounted_branch`
test currently observes zero local claims. Active same-account claim subtraction
is covered only by the private helper test
`active_non_bnb_place_reservation_keeps_discounted_bnb_funding_unresolved`.

The public path cannot create a new active Binance reservation while the future
fee asset is unknown: the owning statement stays unavailable and
`trade.execution.prepare` requires an eligible, current approval. Constructing
or inserting a reservation directly would violate the ticket's no-seeded-finance
rule. Therefore this investigation does not satisfy AC5 and must not be used to
check its acceptance box. The actual Alpaca account/source-use also remains
UNRESOLVED and `FX_DATA_USE_RIGHTS` remains UNVERIFIED; no real provider/account
read or financial action was performed.
