Decision implemented
The user approved allowing a complete, current, account/Proposal-bound conditional fee estimate to qualify the fee/FX review when every independent gate passes. The actual fill asset and amount remain unknown until broker settlement. The chosen branch is immutable for settlement: BNB-current-estimate records only its outgoing BNB claim; received-asset deduction records its fallback asset/amount as non-outgoing allowance; a zero-fee branch admits no positive fee. Unmodeled assets/branches, missing fee truth and excess debit remain reconciliation failures. No source-use status or other RevC gate was promoted.

Implementation
- A complete current fee model plus complete bound BASE/QUOTE/BNB wallet evidence now yields a conditional fee estimate without fabricating `feeCurrency` or `expectedFee`. Required FX/source, wallet, market, risk and permission checks remain separate.
- Prepare stores the approval-bound `SpotFundingPlanPreview` on the reservation alongside the outgoing claims. The schema keeps old reservations backward-compatible and public Prepare rejects renderer-supplied fee plans.
- Settlement validates the saved fee disposition, principal identity, outgoing claim and received-asset fallback allowance before applying broker-reported fees. A changed fee asset/branch or excess cumulative amount fails closed.
- UI copy names `ACTUAL_FEE_ASSET_PENDING`, the no-guarantee limitation and reconciliation behavior.
