Decision

The correction keeps reason SPOT_FEE_CURRENCY_UNKNOWN and feeCurrency=UNKNOWN. The expectedFee field is omitted on the wire when unknown (the Rust Option serializer skips None); it is not serialized as null and no fee amount is fabricated. Independent policy-valuation and monetary-limit checks may pass; they do not qualify the fee statement, grant source-use rights or enable overall Live review.
