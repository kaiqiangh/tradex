# S29.9 — Owning fee evidence and genuinely required execution FX for exact-amount Binance Spot Live PLACE

Parent: [Verify trusted Binance Spot Live execution](https://github.com/kaiqiangh/tradex/issues/121). Starting implementation/review baseline `dev@1f8e231de3fe67aec16db1ae7392f34981617330`. [中文](s29-fee-and-required-fx-spec_zh.md). Planning only; implementation and acceptance have not started.

## Problem Statement

The owning Live PLACE path for an ordinary Binance Spot Live account now decides admission from the venue's own declared capacity and order-rate evidence (S29.8) and it already carries the read-only currency routes and unqualified rates of S28 #120 and the post-fill settlement fees. But the two facts a user must judge before authorizing an exact-amount Spot Live order are still absent from what the owning gate decides.

First, no owning path states the venue's declared fee for this exact account, symbol and side, or the asset the fee is charged in; the only fee the product ever shows is `LiveOrderSettlement.fees`, which exists only after a fill. A user therefore authorizes a spend whose fee is unstated, and the review silently behaves as though the fee were denominated in the quote asset. Second, the delivered FX context is deliberately read-only and portfolio-wide: it names every account balance, position and open-order currency together with the proposal's policy and funding routes, and it permanently leaves `CURRENCY_CONVERSION` `UNAVAILABLE / CURRENCY_CONVERSION_UNAVAILABLE`. Nothing narrows that context to the conversions this placement genuinely needs — the exact expected spend against the declared notional and the authorized maximum — and nothing states the direction, quality, freshness or cost of those conversions for execution. Reading either page does not change what the owning gate decides: the approval review stays eligible and Prepare still proceeds while a genuinely required conversion is missing, an unrelated balance's currency looks like a blocker, and the fee currency is unstated. The delivered evidence is decorative, not authoritative, and parent stories 14, 15 and 23 remain unanswered on the owning path.

## Solution

Make the existing owning Live PLACE path for an ordinary Binance Spot Live account consume one approval-bound **fee statement** and one approval-bound **genuinely-required-execution-FX statement**, both derived from the exact immutable intent and the already-delivered seams, with the same exactness and fail-closed discipline as the delivered slices. For one reviewed exact-amount intent the user sees the venue's declared fee and the exact asset it is charged in, and exactly which conversions this placement genuinely requires — each with its direction, quality, freshness and conservative cost — or the single typed blocker that stops it. Prepare atomically consumes the approval and reserves capacity only when both statements are currently and completely available; otherwise it refuses with the delivered `RISK_EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE` semantics and creates no reservation. Nothing here grants Arm, consent, dispatch or any new execution authority, and the post-fill settlement fee contract, the ACK/fill/CANCEL separation and the rule that a fee is settled exactly once are preserved unchanged.

## User Stories

1. As a user, I want the venue's declared fee and the exact asset it is charged in kept in the original BASE/QUOTE and fee currency, so that USDT is never read as USD and a fee charged in a third asset is never shown as if it were the quote asset.
2. As a user, I want the expected fee derived in the declared fee currency with exact decimal arithmetic and the conservative declared rate, so that no rounding ever shows me a smaller fee than the venue would charge.
3. As a user, I want only the conversions this placement genuinely needs checked, so that a required conversion is never skipped and an unrelated balance, position or open order from another account never fabricates a conversion.
4. As a user, I want each genuinely required conversion to carry its direction, provider quality, provider timestamp, first receipt and conservative cost, so that I can see what the execution actually costs and not only that a rate exists somewhere.
5. As a user, I want a same-currency route treated as exact identity, so that no conversion, rate or parity is invented when no conversion is needed.
6. As a user, I want the fee statement and the required-FX statement bound to the exact immutable Proposal, account, symbol, rule material and source generation, so that another intent's evidence cannot qualify mine.
7. As a user, I want a missing, failed, stale, retired or version-changed fee or rate observation to fail the statement closed with the single typed blocker named, so that yesterday's fee or rate cannot authorize today's exact spend.
8. As a user, I want an unsupported or unbalanced required route to stay explicitly unsupported rather than be replaced by parity or a bridged currency, so that USDT is never silently treated as USD and EURUSD is never inferred from USDEUR.
9. As a user, I want the expected spend and the declared maximum authorized spend shown in their exact units, so that the venue-native QUOTE amount and the workspace-base limit are never conflated.
10. As a user, I want Prepare to consume the approval atomically, reserve capacity and refuse the whole attempt when either statement is not current, so that I never hold a reservation for an exact spend whose fee or required conversion is unstated.
11. As a user, I want the ACK, fill and CANCEL paths kept separate and the settlement fee applied exactly once, so that the pre-trade fee statement is honest evidence and never a second charge or a fabricated release.
12. As a user, I want the captured and pre-arm review to show the fee and required-FX statements that were actually relied on, without renewing them, so that a saved assessment stays honest.
13. As a user, I want a later get, refresh or reopen to be unable to renew a captured fee or FX statement, so that history cannot become fresh authority.
14. As a user, I want the fee currency, the required directions and the binding blocker stated with the numbers, so that I can name what to fix instead of guessing.
15. As a user, I want no renderer-supplied amount, quantity, rate or fee ever accepted, so that the venue's declared figures remain the only source of my fee and conversion evidence.
16. As a user, I want keyboard and 1280/768/390 review to keep the fee, the conversion cost and the single blocker as visible as the amounts, so that financial consent stays deliberate.
17. As a user, I want an exact-amount Spot Live order to remain execution-unqualified even when every fee and rate is current, so that no fee or rate statement is presented as a fill promise, an admission guarantee or a substitute for authenticated preflight.
18. As a reviewer, I want genuine public RED before GREEN, exact-source checks, the ordinary build and independent serial Standards then Spec evidence, so that neither a label nor a fixture-only projection establishes this slice.

## Implementation Decisions

- Extend the existing owning Live PLACE path (risk evaluation → immutable approval review → Prepare → authenticated child Gateway) for ordinary Binance Spot Live accounts only, and only for the exact-amount Market/Limit intent boundary. No parallel authority model, no second fee or FX projection, no second settlement path, no new account, no new saved source or source-selection command, and no renderer-supplied amount, quantity, rate or fee. This slice performs no provider write, no probe order, no Arm, no consent and no dispatch.
- **Consume, never rebuild.** The fee and required-FX statements are derived from already-delivered seams: the S09 account-currency/FX-normalization and FX/stablecoin provenance with its depeg/quality handling (`FR-014`, `FR-065`, `FR-079`, `AC-053`, `AC-066`, `DATA-004`, `DATA-008`, status `IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED`); the S28 #120 delivered `financial_sources::fx_requirements`, `financial_sources::review_context`, the `FxRequirements`/`FxReviewEvidence` shapes and the `RiskDecisionInputKind::CurrencyRates` approval-bound input, together with the delivered `CurrencyRates`/`CURRENCY_CONVERSION_UNAVAILABLE` risk behaviour; the delivered venue-declared commission evidence already obtained by the exact-account authenticated reads of the S29.2 rule source and the S29.5 capacity slice (both read the account response and validate its `commissionRates` as original decimals but deliberately do not project them); the delivered `LiveOrderSettlement.fees`/`feesComplete` and `LiveOrderTradeFact.fees` post-fill contract; and the S29.4/S29.5/S29.6/S29.7/S29.8 rule-material, source-generation, capacity, interval, price-range and owning-admission seams and their bound identities. This slice does not re-read what a delivered slice already obtained, and it does not reshape any delivered projection.
- **Fee statement.** The owning path derives one bounded fee statement for the exact immutable intent from the venue's declared commission evidence and the venue's declared fee-charging asset, read on the existing owned ordinary Binance Live connection under the existing bounded-read discipline: fixed ordinary public host only, no redirect, no Testnet or regional-host fallback and no access bypass; the existing shared IP/UID budget, 512 KiB per-response bound, 30-second deadline and 12-second completion margin; no Control Plane lock held across vault or network I/O; and no new saved source or credential. Where the venue's declared commission evidence is already carried by a delivered read, that evidence is consumed rather than fetched again. The declared maker and taker rates are reported verbatim as exact decimals, and the **expected fee** is derived with the **conservative declared rate** (never the smaller of the two) so that no rounding or rate-basis choice shows a smaller fee than the venue declares. The **fee currency** is the venue's declared fee-charging asset, reported verbatim; when the venue does not genuinely declare it, the fee currency is `UNKNOWN` and the statement fails closed instead of defaulting to the quote asset, the base asset or USD.
- **Genuinely required execution FX.** The required-FX statement is derived from the same delivered route derivation, narrowed to the purposes this placement genuinely needs for the exact selected account: the intent route (the exact expected spend/proceeds currency to the workspace base currency) and, for a BUY only, the intent funding route (the funding unit to the workspace base currency) where each is not an identity route. Account-wide, balance-wide, position-wide and open-order routes for any account — including the selected one — are the portfolio's business and are never this placement's gate. A route is `QUALIFIED` for execution only when the delivered rate for that exact provider pair and direction is present in a current, unexpired observation bound to the same account, source, material generation and requirement material, its provider timestamp and first receipt are within the execution freshness window, and a conservative cost is derivable exactly from its original bid/ask; otherwise the route is `UNQUALIFIED`, `UNSUPPORTED_ROUTE` or unavailable, and the statement fails closed. A route whose two currencies are equal is exact identity and requires no rate.
- **USDT ≠ USD and no parity.** Every currency token is preserved exactly as the venue and the delivered seam declare it. USDT, USD, USDC and the workspace base currency are distinct; no implicit parity, reciprocal, or bridged currency is ever introduced, and EURUSD and USDEUR remain separate directional observations that are never inferred from each other. A required route the delivered bounded producer cannot support (for example USDT to EUR) stays explicitly unsupported and the conversion stays unavailable; it is never rewritten as a supported route.
- **Exactness.** All arithmetic uses `provider_io::decimal`, `provider_io::decimal_cmp` and `provider_io::decimal_subtract` (and the existing exact-multiply helper) only. No `f64`, no floating point, no rounding, and no rounding of a fee or a converted amount in a manner that could make it smaller than the venue's own figure. The exact expected spend is the intent's own notional in its own QUOTE unit; the workspace-base figures exist only when the corresponding required conversion is genuinely qualified.
- **Typed statement, typed blocker.** The fee/required-FX statement carries the exact bound identity and an explicit state enum — `SPOT_FEE_FX_QUALIFIED`, `SPOT_FEE_FX_EVIDENCE_MISMATCH`, `SPOT_FEE_EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`, `SPOT_FEE_CURRENCY_UNKNOWN`, `SPOT_FEE_RATE_UNSUPPORTED`, `SPOT_REQUIRED_FX_EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`, `SPOT_REQUIRED_FX_UNQUALIFIED` and `SPOT_REQUIRED_FX_UNSUPPORTED_ROUTE` — together with the declared fee rate basis actually used, the declared fee currency and its declared origin, the exact expected fee in that currency, the exact expected spend in QUOTE, the workspace base currency, the workspace-base expected spend and fee only when the required conversions qualified, at most two bounded required-route entries (purpose, from/to currency, direction, provider pair, quality, provider timestamp, first receipt, conservative cost), the carried provenance limitations, and the two bound evidence-version digests. The single binding blocker is a typed reason, never free text. The consumed slices' standing provenance labels are carried verbatim and never promoted into a second, hidden gate; the fatal set is exactly unknown or incomplete fee/route evidence.
- **Owning consumption.** A non-`QUALIFIED` fee/FX statement contributes exactly one typed blocker to the immutable approval review, so the review is not eligible and the existing Prepare eligibility path refuses with `RISK_EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE` and creates no reservation and no partial state; no second, divergent fee or FX check is added. The existing `CURRENCY_CONVERSION` check becomes `PASS` only for the genuinely required routes that are genuinely qualified, and otherwise keeps its delivered `UNAVAILABLE / CURRENCY_CONVERSION_UNAVAILABLE` meaning. A same-currency-only intent that requires no conversion still requires the fee statement. The owning gate is always evaluated against a freshly derived statement, so one that has since expired can never be replayed from the reviewed decision; a genuine change to the fee or rate evidence changes the review digest and leaves the review ineligible, so re-admission requires a newly reviewed approval.
- **Settlement unchanged.** The pre-trade fee statement is evidence only. The delivered ACK / fill / CANCEL separation, the exactly-once fee settlement (`LiveOrderSettlement.fees` / `feesComplete`, `LiveOrderTradeFact.fees`), the reservation-release-only-for-capacity-actually-released rule, the unknown-PLACE reconciliation and the provider-read-never-releases-a-local-reservation rule are all inherited unchanged, and this slice must not weaken them.
- **Execution-unqualified.** The statement never grants Arm, consent, dispatch or any new authority, even when every number is current. It is not a fill promise, not a fee or conversion guarantee for the venue's later taker phase, and not a substitute for rights, quotes, permissions, health, funding, dynamic admission, Arm, consent, Prepare, Gateway or immediate authenticated preflight.
- **Contract and pairing.** The generated wire contract must admit exactly the values this contract emits: both bound evidence versions are `sha256:` digests declared at the 71 characters of a `sha256:` value, exactly as `proposal_hash` already is, because React decodes every response through the generated validators before any component reads it. The paired English/Chinese financial, wire and evidence contracts stay synchronized. Prototype code remains unchanged.
- **AC-035 disposition.** This slice is the owning home for the **fee-estimate and expected/maximum-authorized-spend portions of `AC-035`** on the exact-amount ordinary Binance Spot Live path: the expected spend and the declared maximum authorized spend are shown in their exact units, and the fee estimate is shown in the declared fee currency together with the workspace-base equivalent where the required conversion is genuinely qualified. The **bid/ask/spread/quote-age portions of `AC-035` remain with the quote/depth owning slice** (S29.3 #124 and the delivered quote-freshness producers) and are not claimed here. The **variable-spend market-order path is not this slice's** and remains separate owning work, because an exact-amount intent is where a pre-authorized maximum spend is meaningful. `AC-035` as a whole therefore stays `NOT_STARTED` and is **not** claimed closed; its requirement-matrix row should add this slice for the fee and spend portions only. This slice does not modify any other requirement row.
- **Gates stay OPEN.** Parent #121, the map gate #1, the S28 gate, S17, the physical S27 sleep waiver, the S27/S28 financial gates and the `dev`→`main` merge remain OPEN, and the still-in-progress S29.3/S29.5 producer acceptances are unchanged. Nothing in this slice claims any of them closed.

## Testing Decisions

Use the existing public account/source/draft/Proposal/rules/capacity/interval/risk/approval/prepare flow and actual React, with only the external HTTP/vault producers fake. Record genuine public RED before GREEN for each new owned behaviour. Read the delivered `live_place_preparation_rejects_missing_cross_currency_fx_without_partial_state` test first and preserve its boundary: a missing genuinely-required conversion still makes Prepare refuse with `RISK_EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`, creates no reservation, leaves the Proposal at `NEEDS_APPROVAL` and leaves the approval `ISSUED`. This slice extends that boundary by consuming a current fee statement and a route-scoped required-FX statement; it never relaxes it.

Verify, at the public seam: a current, completely available declared fee (with its declared currency) and a qualified genuinely-required conversion qualifying the exact intent, with the conservative declared rate used for the expected fee; an intent whose required route is unsupported or unqualified failing closed with the single typed blocker named and Prepare refusing with no reservation and no partial state; a same-currency-only intent adding no conversion gate while still requiring the fee statement; a fee declared in an asset that is neither the quote nor the base asset reported verbatim and never remapped to the quote asset or USD; USDT to a non-USD base reported as an unsupported route and never rewritten as a supported USD route, and EURUSD never inferred from USDEUR; an unrelated account's balance, position or open-order currency never becoming this placement's gate; a stale, failed, retired or version-changed fee or rate observation retiring the statement and leaving the review ineligible; a renderer-supplied amount, quantity, rate or fee rejected; and a genuine change to the venue's declared fee or rate changing the review digest and leaving the review ineligible so that re-admission needs a newly reviewed approval. Confirm every positive case still grants no Arm, consent, dispatch or any new authority, and that the captured and pre-arm review display only the statement that was actually relied on, without renewal on a later get, refresh or reopen. The retirement, staleness, pending/failure, binding, rule-material, source-generation, reopen, newer-owner and per-account isolation behaviour of the consumed slices, and the delivered settlement/fill/reconciliation and fee-settled-exactly-once behaviour, are inherited unchanged and stay covered by their delivered suites; this slice must not weaken them and must never renew a captured statement.

Replay the actual current, captured and pre-arm views plus the representative rejection and recovery paths at keyboard 1280/768/390 without overflow. Reaching the positive case through the real app requires the external producer to be able to declare the exact account's fee evidence and a qualified rate for the one genuinely required route; both are external HTTP producer states, so the fixture seam gains them and no product assertion or Control Plane value is relaxed to reach either.

Require the current full and affected rule/capacity/interval/price-range/owning/Hot/Gateway/settlement suites, generated IPC and paired traceability, the ordinary desktop build and pin, independent serial Standards then Spec review, and exact source/evidence/remote/tracker handoff. Native, provider, financial, physical, prototype and `main` evidence remain separately labelled and unclaimed.

These remain later owning work, not waived.
