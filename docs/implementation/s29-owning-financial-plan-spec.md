## Problem Statement

The user can collect an exact-symbol commission declaration, but still cannot review a complete financial plan for the immutable Spot intent. The owning statement consumes historical account comparison rates, emits no monetary fee, and cannot qualify required USDT valuation. Principal capacity and settlement each currently track one asset. Consequently the complete live path lacks a consistent account/intent-bound explanation of fees, funding, workspace values and conditional BNB exposure, followed by atomic multi-asset reservations and exact settlement.

The research also exposed a financial contract ambiguity: a current estimate, an authorized allowance, a broker-enforced bound and a realized fee are different facts. A SELL limit constrains the minimum price, not maximum proceeds or fees. A separate local maximum-spend field is not an additional Binance order parameter. A spot quote is not proof of Binance's internal average BNB fee-conversion rate. Resolving these differences is part of this item, not a reason to waive the full goal or substitute permanent refusal for implementation.

## Solution

Provide one owning financial plan, derived in the trusted Control Plane from the unchanged Proposal and genuine current producers. It includes complete three-family fees and charging branches, exact BASE/QUOTE/BNB funds, the purpose-specific conversions actually consumed, native order bounds and the independently reviewed monetary authorization. The same plan supplies monetary risk, the immutable review and Prepare. Complete supported inputs must produce genuine positive financial qualification through the public product seam; incomplete or contradictory inputs produce a typed blocker without partial authority or reservation.

Prepare consumes the approval and reserves the complete native asset vector in one transaction. Gateway preflight revalidates the approved financial material before transmission. Real fills and fees settle each asset exactly once; pending and unknown work retains its capacity. This plan never grants Arm, market-data rights, account permissions, approval, dispatch or a future fill/fee guarantee on its own.

### Resolved monetary model and authorization decisions — still unimplemented

These decisions resolve the former monetary/BNB design questions from the authoritative PRD §44 and existing UI §14.2 distinction between TradeX authorization/reservation and broker enforcement. They specify the model; they do not establish source rights, qualify present observations, change the normative wire or relax any current live gate. Current source-only history remains historical. Implementation must synchronize the paired normative contracts before exposing new financial eligibility.

1. **Supported intent forms.** Preserve the existing exact Spot boundary: BASE MARKET BUY/SELL with existing DAY semantics; BUY-only QUOTE MARKET where provider metadata permits it; BASE LIMIT GTC/IOC/FOK. Unsupported forms, including QUOTE SELL, do not silently become another order. The current model prices BASE LIMIT at the immutable limit, BASE MARKET at the relevant genuine contemporaneous best ask/bid with the existing displayed-size/slippage guards, and BUY QUOTE MARKET at the native quote amount. For the latter, modeled received BASE is the rational quote amount divided by the ask. This is a full-fill scenario, not an exact predicted base fill. A SELL limit scenario is minimum-price proceeds, not a maximum fee/proceeds envelope.
2. **Model class.** Use `CURRENT_ORDER_SCENARIO`, with method version `SPOT_THREE_FAMILY_RATIONAL_CEIL18_V1`. It is an exact calculation under recorded quantity/price/role/rate/conversion assumptions, followed by outward model rounding. It is neither a venue rounding simulation nor an absolute future fee bound. Preserve explicit `VENUE_PER_FILL_ROUNDING_UNPROVEN` and, when BNB modeling consumes a price, `BNB_INTERNAL_AVERAGE_EQUIVALENCE_UNPROVEN`. These are model limitations, not missing inputs to a purported guaranteed-fee calculation; no guaranteed-fee calculation is offered. An unavailable required model input remains a blocker.
3. **Rational calculation and output.** Keep positive decimal ratios as exact integer numerator/denominator through the complete family expression; do not truncate an intermediate base quantity, inverse price or conversion. Round each final native family liability upward to18 fractional places, then add its three rounded liabilities exactly. For received-asset deductions, subtract that complete rounded model fee from the exact gross-receipt expression, then round the net receipt downward to18 places; the displayed net must reconcile with the displayed model fee rather than a different unrounded fee. Preserve full wire/display values; normalized numeric outputs must fit the existing64-character monetary bound, otherwise return a typed unavailable-model reason. Denominator zero, negative/invalid required values or arithmetic/resource bounds fail closed. The legacy decimal division floors to18 places and must not be used as an upper-rational division or followed by a ceiling that cannot recover its discarded remainder. The18-place scale is an explicit TradeX model scale, not inferred native commission precision.
4. **Complete role selection per charging branch.** For each allowed role and the selected side, calculate and outward-round all three family expressions using that role's standard/tax/special plus side terms. Select the largest complete rounded fallback model total for the fallback branch, and independently the largest complete rounded discounted model total for the BNB branch. The selected roles can differ because only standard is weighted by the returned multiplier. Retain each branch's selected role and original family inputs; never sum independent family maxima, reuse the fallback winner for BNB, or double-add side terms. Compare those complete model totals as exact decimals; deterministic equal totals use TAKER as the display tie-break without claiming that a taker fill occurred. Keep the pre-rounding expressions separately for audit: their winner is not assumed to remain the winner after per-family outward rounding. Retain both roles for ordinary LIMIT; MARKET may use TAKER only under the pinned supported native MARKET contract. Mixed LIMIT roles are represented by the largest complete role scenario, with per-fill rounding still explicitly unproven.
5. **BNB current-price model.** The selected source profile must expose an explicit received-asset→BNB valuation path. For a permitted same-quote-currency pair of source products, the model ratio is the received-asset ask divided by BNB bid. This selects the high cost ratio in the displayed bid/ask rectangle; it does not claim to bound the exchange's internal average. Each source, direction, leg, size/amount applicability, unit and time is recorded. Discounted standard uses the returned multiplier; tax and special are not discounted. No avgPrice/spot quote is labeled the exact engine conversion, no funding transaction is created and no reciprocal/bridge is enabled merely because numbers are available. The executable provider/path selection remains the open source duty below.
6. **Conditional funds decision.** Compare exact-account BNB free minus active local BNB claims against the complete current modeled BNB allowance. Required holdings are not inferred from flags. With active discount and a proven zero effective BNB balance, a strictly positive discounted fee expression establishes current fallback without a BNB price lookup; the inactive BNB amount remains absent, with a typed `ZERO_BNB_CURRENT_FALLBACK` basis rather than zero-valued fabricated conversion. If the complete discounted expression is exactly zero, record that algebraic fact without inventing a paid asset or requiring a pointless rate. Otherwise a positive balance requires the valid conversion model to decide sufficiency; missing conversion cannot assert either sufficiency or fallback. Insufficient modeled BNB selects the full received-asset fallback; sufficient modeled BNB selects the BNB funding claim. Both branches remain disclosed as current conditions, never a guaranteed future charging asset. A modeled fallback exceeding its gross receipt is unsupported until its true additional-funding semantics are known, rather than a negative receipt or hidden extra debit.
7. **Approval and the hard local limit.** The financial approval freezes the model, required routes, exact native principal allowance, any outgoing BNB fee-funding allowance, fee values, budget units and conditional branches in the existing immutable review digest. The renderer cannot supply those trusted amounts. For BUY MARKET, the existing immutable QUOTE `maximumSpend` bounds the total current modeled outgoing cost: principal plus actually outgoing fee funding valued through an explicitly approved quote-valuation path. Received-BASE fallback fees reduce incoming BASE and are not added again to outgoing QUOTE. Subtract the approved modeled outgoing fee value to derive the remaining QUOTE principal allowance; refuse if the current required principal/total exceeds either allowance. For BUY QUOTE MARKET the native principal claim is its fixed quoteOrderQty, not the larger local total-cost ceiling. For BUY BASE MARKET the local principal allowance is reserved, and the UI explicitly states that Binance receives quantity rather than a maximum-spend parameter. For BUY LIMIT, quantity×limit supplies the native principal bound; the review separately freezes any modeled BNB allowance and its total quote value. An explicitly supplied local maximum must still cover that current total; absent a separate maximum, the user reviews the derived principal bound plus the separately disclosed modeled fee allowance. No allowance is issued before the existing explicit approval action.
8. **SELL and policy units.** A BASE SELL authorizes and funds its BASE quantity. Its received-QUOTE fee reduces proceeds; a consumed BNB fee creates a separate native claim. Preserve the existing MARKET maximum-value check as a TradeX pre-submission reviewed trade-value ceiling, and label it accordingly; it does not cap future sale proceeds, establish a QUOTE cash debit or become a broker price parameter. The BNB allowance is separately visible in the same approval. Do not compare a BASE funding claim with a QUOTE ceiling. Order/daily-notional policies consume gross trade value; reserved-capital policies consume the outgoing principal/fee vector once; received-asset fees and incoming assets are not duplicate outgoing claims. Other enabled position/exposure/loss policies retain their real evidence requirements. No current-value guard guarantees future favorable SELL proceeds or future fees.
9. **Preflight and financial material.** Prepare and Gateway compare freshly computed required native amounts and values with the immutable approved allowances, in addition to all existing exact account/source/policy/clock/quote-validity checks. Exceeding a hard local allowance rejects submission; unavailable inputs refuse it. Genuine changed rates, quotes, depth, conditions, amounts, source identity/generation or validity invalidate incompatible consent. Only numerically identical decimal formatting and collection receipt/digest changes may be excluded from the financial-material comparison; their original audit provenance still persists separately. IDs/symbols, policy/source/license versions and unknown-active obligations are never normalized into equality. Matching material does not extend consent/observation expiry or replace the original capture. No new tolerance for changed price/depth is introduced.
10. **Settlement beyond a model.** If broker truth exceeds an approved modeled allowance, record every actual native debit and the uncovered difference rather than clipping to the allowance or retroactively approving it. Degrade/disarm and reconcile the affected account through the existing recovery path; retire current financial observations. Cumulative exact execution/fee identity drives once-only settlement. Pending/unknown or incomplete fee evidence retains unresolved claims. Terminal confirmation releases only unused authorized capacity and never fabricates negative remaining capacity, extra release or zero unobserved fee. Restored readiness requires new authoritative account/funding evidence; stale pre-fill funds cannot be reused. This is recovery from realized truth, not a promised cap on future fills or permission to dispatch known excess current spend.
11. **Reservation proof scope.** Ordinary preview and Prepare consider all other active native claims. Revalidation of an already prepared attempt must recognize its own held vector from the trusted durable attempt/Gateway context, compare required amounts with that held vector and current broker free, and subtract other outstanding local holds once; never add its claim to broker free or charge its own hold a second time. No renderer-supplied ignore-claim/attempt list is accepted. A broker aggregate locked amount or ACK alone cannot prove which local claim is already represented. Proven overlap requires exact current provider/account/order/asset evidence; unknown overlap retains its hold and is disclosed as capacity uncertainty. If only a conservative lower bound is established, sufficient funds may be proved within that bound, while failure to fit the bound is not misreported as an exact provider deficit. Reading reflection evidence does not release or settle the reservation. Required local funding below zero or an unproved overlap never manufactures new available capacity.

The original S29.9 sentence requiring an estimate never to understate any future venue charge is not a theorem established by RevC, known precision or a read-only declaration. This item's chosen model preserves estimated fees and the hard **pre-submission** spend check required by PRD §44; it does not silently turn the model into a future exact-charge guarantee. Stronger broker-enforced bounds are disclosed only for native parameters that actually enforce them. No source-only acceptance is used to claim this implemented.
