## Remaining scope definitions

- `VARIANTS`: Remaining applicable loading/empty/stale/failed/blocked/disabled/completed and interruption/race variants need explicit per-case reconciliation.
- `PROVIDER`: Real provider truth/integration remains separate and skipped/open.
- `MODEL`: Real model/gateway login, recovery or inference acceptance is not established by the synthetic/passive record.
- `PERMISSIONS`: Other transfer/custody/unsupported margin/leverage permission variants and native secure entry remain open.
- `HISTORY`: Populated Saved/archived history, loading/failure/retry remain open.
- `ANNOUNCEMENTS`: Broader live-region transitions and streaming-noise inspection remain open.
- `PHYSICAL`: Physical sleep/wake/clock evidence remains skipped/open; synthetic startup is not a substitute.
- `LIFECYCLE`: Complete terminal/fill/settlement/account-position refresh and interruption/reconciliation chains remain open.
- `SPOT`: Qualified positive owning Spot approval/Prepare remains under #135 and independent financial gates.
- `RESTART`: Full process-restart restoration and dispatch interruption remain open.
- `RESOLUTION`: Complete provider evidence and sufficient-absence rules remain unavailable/open.
- `IMPORT`: Native picker, actual desktop entry and restore publication are not proved by this browser shim.
- `RATE_LIMIT`: Actual producer Retry-After, cooldown timing, backoff and reconciliation scheduling are not proved by browser injection.
- `ERROR_BROWSER`: All 20 canonical error variants have not been exercised individually in the browser.
- `RECEIPT`: A dedicated current runtime receipt for this specific screen is not present in this index; retain its bounded prose/script evidence and capture a final replay receipt.
